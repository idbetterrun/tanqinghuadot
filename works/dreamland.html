dreamland

a little text world you can poke at, run by an llm

still in the early stages · from june 2026

worlds and stories grow slowly.

dreamland is meant to be a tool that helps you build a whole world from nothing.

you type one line. it first asks you two or three things that genuinely change where things go, then hands you a draft you can start playing: a few hard rules, a few factions, one live tension. after that, every change you push out comes back as a response — who it touches, why, what it leaves behind. you review it, uncheck what you don't want, and only what you confirm counts. confirmed things enter the timeline and turn into people, events, ledgers and old grudges; dozens of turns later, some later simulation digs them back up. that's how a world gets thick.

history can't be edited either, only rewritten with a trace — if you want to change something that already happened, the old record doesn't vanish; it hangs behind the new one.

the cost stays low: one turn sends exactly one model call, and the context fed in is capped, so a world can sprawl without the bill sprawling with it.

tauri + rust + sqlite on the back end, react up front, a macos desktop app, everything stored locally.
