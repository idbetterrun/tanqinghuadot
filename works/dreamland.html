dreamland

a macos app for building and exploring fictional worlds with ai

still in the early stages · from june 2026

worlds and stories grow slowly.

dreamland is a local-first macos app for people who like making up worlds and writing stories. you build up characters, factions and rules, then explore how the world responds when something happens.

start with a seed of an idea. ai asks follow-up questions and helps you shape a first draft: the world's rules, its main factions, a tension, somewhere for the story to begin. you review and confirm it, and the world takes shape.

from there, you can add details or ask it to play out an event: who reacts, what changes, what trouble is left brewing. the results wait for you to review, select or discard; only what you confirm becomes part of the world. it moves when you add something, request a simulation or advance time.

characters, events, facts and relationships stay in the world. you can browse them, follow the timeline and see what changed in each version; later simulations draw on relevant records. if you revise an earlier event or fact, you can check its downstream effects and decide what to keep, rewrite or remove. the earlier record stays traceable.

world data stays on your mac. you connect your own ai provider, and generation sends your input and relevant world context to it. built with tauri, rust, sqlite and react; still in the early stages.
