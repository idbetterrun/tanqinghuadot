notiee

an ios notes app that turns photos, voice notes and text into a knowledge base you can search and ask

coming soon to App Store · TestFlight open now · iOS 18.0+

photos get read into a title, summary, key points and keywords, with any todos pulled out. voice notes transcribe live on the phone — pause and resume, play back at speed, tap a line to jump to that moment. markdown just saves.

the ai handles ocr, summaries, the longer write-up, key points, term definitions and todo extraction. todos and events it finds go straight into your task list and calendar — system events, notiee events, ai-scheduled blocks and imported timetables share one timeline, with reminders and a live countdown on the lock screen and in the dynamic island. full-text or semantic search finds something even when you only remember the gist.

notti is the ai companion living in your library: eighteen skills, from finding notes and tidying key points to creating todos, editing records, checking your schedule and searching the web. deletions ask you first, every step can be undone, and the memory it keeps of your preferences and long-term goals stays on the device.

your data stays on your iphone by default — a note can be encrypted (encrypted content stays out of search and sync), the app locks with a passcode or face id, and a note exports as .tmn with its text and images or the whole library backs up to icloud drive by hand. the free version gives you 30 notes a month on cloud models, 300 with a subscription, then falls back to on-device ocr; notiee+ is a one-time purchase with your own endpoint and key and no monthly ceiling. simplified chinese, traditional chinese and english.
