two destinations now, Today and Records. pull down on Today to shoot, pull up to record, and notes, todos and events all land in the same library. a note is a title plus one free markdown body — the formatting toolbar is always there, it autosaves 600ms after you stop typing, and AI organization never overwrites what you wrote by hand. a record can carry several attachments.

the camera got rebuilt: 4:3 framing, zoom presets generated from the lenses your device actually has, real tap-to-focus, live photos, bursts up to 9 and draft recovery. recording starts from the pull-up on Today and transcribes live on device — pause and resume, a 30-minute limit, speed playback, tap a transcript line to jump to that moment. everything you capture goes through one model call that produces title, summary, body and todos, in the background while you do something else; a screenshot shortcut can drop captures in without opening the app.

Notti is the AI companion living in your library. answers cite your notes and web pages with tappable sources; it keeps a local, encrypted memory of your preferences and long-term goals, never synced to iCloud; and Notti emergence surfaces connections between notes you might have forgotten. ask it for a markdown document and it becomes a card you can preview, share or add to a record. the free app runs all AI through notiee's backend, so there's no key to fill in; notiee+ is a one-time purchase that talks straight to your own model provider.

on the free app, sign in with an email code or Apple, and one shared AI allowance covers every feature — no more counting captures per month. the tiers are Leaf and Folio; Folio is currently closed to new subscriptions, while existing subscriptions, restores and entitlement delivery keep working. notiee+ stays a one-time purchase with your own key. your data lives on your iPhone: encrypted notes stay out of search and sync, the app locks with a passcode or Face ID, a note exports as .tmn, and the whole library can back up to iCloud Drive by hand. simplified chinese, traditional chinese and english.
