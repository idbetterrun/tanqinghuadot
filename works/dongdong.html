dongdongplanet

a movement-rewards app for kids

TestFlight beta is open, july 2026–present

a little movement today, a little further into the stars.

dongdongplanet is an iphone app that turns daily movement into a planet-hopping adventure for kids. finish an activity, earn energy and work towards the next planet. each day's effort becomes another step in a journey they can see.

each day starts with three missions: follow-along exercises and activities to do with family. some are for kids to try on their own, while others need an adult alongside them. movement can be something you do together.

the journey begins on launch star, then continues through coral star, golden star, bluewind star and sprout star. the star map shows where they've been and what's ahead, while their little companion diandian joins them along the way.

the sports area brings together jump-rope tests, weekly challenges and milestones. earned fragments can be exchanged in the weekly shop for collectibles and dress-up items. the review area keeps past activity records, so kids can look back at what they've done.

parents have their own space to manage children's profiles, view exercise plans and handle privacy settings. permissions and sensitive settings sit with the grown-ups, leaving kids a simpler space to move, explore and collect small achievements.

if this version isn't quite what you had in mind, there's more to look forward to. a new version of dongdongplanet with a completely redesigned UI built with Flutter is coming soon, with an Android version launching alongside it.
