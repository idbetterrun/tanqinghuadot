i changed a few things on the works page over the past few days. two new projects, a few rewritten introductions, and two discontinued projects moved out of the list. put together, it felt like enough for a little update.

## dreamland has a new shape

in july's talks post, i wrote that dreamland started with my habit of making up settings and watching stories grow inside them. the website can finally describe it a little more clearly now: a macOS app for building and exploring fictional worlds with AI.

start with a seed of a world, then gradually add characters, factions and rules. drop an event into it and see who responds, what changes, and what trouble it leaves behind. AI's suggestions wait for your review; only what you confirm becomes part of the world.

characters, events, facts and relationships stay on record, with a timeline to look back through. when you revise something in the past, you can check what it affected and decide what to keep or rewrite. compared with one long chat, this gives the world a set of records you can actually browse.

world data stays on your Mac, and you connect your own AI provider. the site now has [beta downloads and a GitHub link](/works/dreamland.html), too. still early, but its shape is a little clearer this time.

## a diner between worlds

another new face is [no smiting at the diner](/works/nsad.html). first person, deliberately rough low-poly 3D, a neutral diner tucked between other worlds. one house rule: no spells or fighting.

heroes, demon lords, ghosts and aliens all need dinner. they won't always tell you plainly what they want, though. you listen for clues and answer with a dish. misreading someone's feelings might open up a different story, too.

there's plenty to handle outside the kitchen. carry plates through a crowded room, keep track of who's about to pay and who's looking for the exit, and stop sworn enemies from fighting. if they actually start, grab the fire extinguisher — the job description for running a diner seems a little broad.

this project is also in its early stages. there's a small scene in the introduction that i like: a demon lord might just want an ordinary bowl of egg fried rice, the way his mum made it. leaving room for moments like that in a noisy management game feels good.

## dongdongplanet, and a notiee status update

[dongdongplanet](/works/dongdong.html) has joined works, too. it's for kids, turning daily movement into a journey from planet to planet: finish an activity, earn energy, get a little closer to the next stop. diandian comes along for the adventure, while parents have their own space for profiles, exercise plans and privacy settings.

dongdongplanet's TestFlight beta is currently open. meanwhile, [notiee's](/works/notiee.html) current TestFlight round has ended, and the website's entry now explains that the next round is still to come. its App Store status is still coming soon; i'll update that when there's more to share.

## two apples count as an update, too

artista and tomanotes are discontinued. this time, i moved them out of the project list and onto the tree in the bottom right, as two clickable apples. tap one and you can still read what it was and why development stopped.

tomanotes was the first thing i made that strangers actually downloaded. it's planned to become part of notiee desktop. artista aimed to be a Mac app for building websites by dragging things around; eventually i realised my abilities couldn't carry the idea yet, and Codex was already doing something very close.

they both still have a place on this website. if someone happens to click an apple on the tree, they'll get to know the two little things that once grew here.

that's roughly what changed on the site these past few days. new projects, older projects finding a new shape, and a couple staying where they are for now. thanks for stopping by, and for clicking through to see how these little worlds are doing.
